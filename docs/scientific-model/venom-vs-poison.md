# Venom vs Poison

Venom Atlas distinguishes biological delivery routes and material classes.

- Venom: biologically produced toxic material delivered through a structure such as a stinger.
- Poison: toxic effect generally resulting from ingestion, contact, or passive exposure.
- Secretion: broad biological output category that may or may not be toxic.
- Isolated toxin: purified or identified molecule from a larger biological mixture.

For _Solenopsis invicta_, the scaffold models exposure as sting delivery, often with a bite-and-sting sequence where mandibles anchor and the stinger injects venom.

This scaffold avoids equating all observed clinical outcomes with one molecular entity.
